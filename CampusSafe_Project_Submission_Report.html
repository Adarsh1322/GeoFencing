Executive Summary & Real-World Need

1 Executive Summary: The Real-World Need

1.1 The Reality of Sprawling Indian Campuses

Modern educational institutions in India—exemplified by Lovely Professional University (LPU), which encompasses over 600 acres with multi-storey academic blocks, vast residential hostels, sports complexes, libraries, and transit corridors—host tens of thousands of students moving daily across varying schedules. While campus security checkpoints exist at main entry gates, student safety during evening hours, inter-block transit, and lab commutes presents real operational challenges: sudden health distress, accidents in secluded zones, or night-time security anxieties.

1.2 The Dilemma: Invasive Surveillance vs. Helplessness

Existing approaches to student safety suffer from two critical flaws:

Flaw 1: Invasive Commercial Surveillance Trackers
Privacy Hazard
Apps like Life360 or social trackers run 24/7 background location feeds, treating students like suspects and draining smartphone batteries. Crucially, allowing classmates or peers to see each other’s live whereabouts introduces dangerous risks of peer stalking, coercion, and unwanted monitoring.
Flaw 2: The "Zero Network" Breakdown
Operational Failure
Conventional mobile applications depend entirely on steady 4G/5G internet. The moment a student enters a basement lab, an elevator, or a dense hostel dead zone, traditional tracking apps fail immediately with “Network Error”, dropping emergency signals and losing track precisely when danger is highest.

1.3 The CampusSafe Solution

CampusSafe is an offline-first, privacy-respecting student safety and geofencing companion. It operates on an unshakeable philosophy: Your smartphone protects you even when the network fails, and you remain in sovereign control of your privacy at all times.

System Philosophy & Core Architecture

2 System Philosophy & Architecture

2.1 The Four Non-Negotiable Pillars

CampusSafe was engineered under four strict operational boundaries to ensure ethical, reliable safety delivery:

1. Consent is Sovereign (No Passive Surveillance)
Privacy First
No proctor, professor, warden, or guardian can ever remotely “turn on” a student’s tracking. Location is collected only when the student actively taps “Start Safe Journey”. When not in a journey, the tracking hardware is completely powered down.
2. Offline-First Resilience (The Device is a Vault)
Reliability
Network disconnection does not invalidate an active journey. Telemetry is saved directly to the device’s internal SQLite database with monotonic sequence numbering. When connectivity returns, the phone automatically synchronizes stored data.
3. Emergency SOS is Paramount
Life Safety
The one-tap SOS button triggers instant local sirens and stores incident coordinates with top priority. Even if pressed inside a signal-dead basement, the emergency packet is queued and fired to the cloud the microsecond connectivity flickers back.
4. Strict Anti-Stalking Policy
Security
Classmates, friends, and peers have zero visibility into each other’s location. Live coordinates are only visible to the university security room during active journeys and to verified guardians if the student explicitly turns guardian sharing ON.

3 The Complete Journey: Phase I (Setup & Start)

To understand how CampusSafe functions in everyday life, let us walk through the journey of Aarav, an MCA student at Lovely Professional University, as he attends classes, walks across campus at night, and returns safely.

Stage 1: Verified Campus Onboarding
Identity Security
Aarav downloads the CampusSafe app on his Android phone and registers with his university email and roll number.
  • Anti-Impersonation Gate: New student accounts are placed in “Pending Approval” status until verified by LPU administration, preventing rogue accounts.
  • Campus Association: Once verified, Aarav’s account is linked to Lovely Professional University, automatically configuring local campus coordinates and boundary geofences.
Stage 2: Adding a Guardian (Peace of Mind Without Loss of Privacy)
Guardian Lifecycle
Aarav wants his mother to know he is safe when traveling between campus blocks late in the evening.
  • Aarav generates a secure, single-use 6-digit invitation link inside the app and shares it with his mother via messaging.
  • His mother consumes the link on her phone. No paid third-party SMS gateways are required.
  • Dual Consent Protection: Even though his mother is linked, she cannot see his location unless Aarav starts an active journey and leaves the “Share with Guardian” toggle ON.
Stage 3: Starting a “Safe Journey” at 8:30 PM
Journey Initiation
At 8:30 PM, Aarav leaves the Central Library (Block 34) to walk 1.2 kilometers across campus to Boys Hostel 4. He opens CampusSafe and taps “Start Safe Journey”.
  • The app contacts the server to verify campus hours and boundary rules, then creates an authorized journey lease.
  • An Android Foreground Safety Service launches immediately, displaying a clear persistent notification: “CampusSafe Active • Protecting You”.
  • The app displays a live map showing Aarav’s position, the LPU geofence circle, and an active safety countdown timer.
Stage 4: Mathematical Geofencing & Automated Attendance
Smart Geofence
During academic daytime hours, the exact same system verifies whether Aarav is present for lecture attendance.
  • The app executes the high-precision Haversine spherical distance formula directly on the smartphone.
  • If distance ≤ 100 meters from the lecture hall, punch-in is allowed with a green badge; if > 100 meters, it is blocked. This completely eliminates proxy attendance and fake GPS spoofing.

Continuing Aarav’s walk across Lovely Professional University, we observe how the system handles extreme real-world challenges: network blackouts, screen locks, emergency distress, and safe completion.

Stage 5: Walking Through Network Dead Zones (The Zero-Signal Test)
Offline Vault
Halfway through his walk, Aarav enters a dense pathway between multi-storey academic blocks where cellular 4G/5G signals drop completely to zero.
  • Normal apps fail here: Other applications freeze, display error dialogs, or stop tracking.
  • CampusSafe continues seamlessly: The app detects the absence of internet and quietly shifts into offline vault mode. High-accuracy GPS fixes are recorded every few seconds into local SQLite with atomic sequence numbers (1, 2, 3...). Not a single meter of his path is lost.
Stage 6: Pocket Mode & Android Doze Mode Protection
OS Resilience
Aarav locks his phone screen and puts it in his pocket. Android enters aggressive power-saving “Doze Mode”.
  • Because CampusSafe runs a certified Android Foreground Service with high-priority location privileges, the operating system never terminates or freezes the tracker.
  • A convenient “Stop Journey” button remains pinned in the notification shade, allowing Aarav to conclude his journey without even unlocking his phone.
Stage 7: The Emergency Scenario: 1-Tap SOS Lifeline
SOS Lifeline
Suppose Aarav notices an injured student or encounters a dangerous situation. He taps the prominent red SOS Button.
  • Immediate Action: The phone sounds a loud local siren and locks the emergency coordinates into storage with status EMERGENCY_PENDING.
  • Security Desk Alarm: On the LPU Security Control Room Dashboard, a red banner flashes, an audible siren sounds, and Aarav’s coordinates pulse on the live map.
  • Offline Guarantee: Even if pressed inside a signal-dead basement, the emergency packet is queued and fired to the server the microsecond connectivity flickers back.
Stage 8: Safe Arrival & Automated Reconciliation
Safe Completion
Aarav arrives safely at his hostel gate and taps “Stop Journey”.
  • The native tracking service stops instantly. GPS hardware powers off to preserve battery life.
  • As his phone connects to hostel Wi-Fi, the app’s outbox worker flushes all stored location breadcrumbs to the cloud database in a single neat batch.
  • The server marks the session “Safely Concluded”, his mother receives a peaceful confirmation notification, and the live location stream terminates.

Engineering Rigor & Failure Hardening

6 Engineering Rigor: Hardening Worst-Case Failures

Safety-critical software cannot rely on “happy path” assumptions. During Phase 2 engineering, we conducted an exhaustive failure audit to identify how the system could break under extreme real-world conditions, fixing three confirmed Priority-0 (P0) vulnerabilities.

Defect P0-1: Safe Logout & Identity Isolation
Fixed
The Real-World Hazard: On shared family or roommate phones, if Student A logged out while a journey was active and Student B logged in, the native Android tracking service would continue running anonymously and attribute Student A’s GPS points to Student B.
The Hardening Fix: Added a pre-logout safety hook in AuthContext.js. The app halts the native Android service, unregisters location hardware, and cleanly isolates un-uploaded records so another student’s account can never access or upload them.
Defect P0-2: Guaranteed “Stop Journey” Notification Delivery
Fixed
The Real-World Hazard: When a student tapped “Stop” on the Android notification drawer while the phone’s JavaScript engine was asleep or the network was offline, the stop notice was never sent to the server, leaving the student marked as “actively walking” forever.
The Hardening Fix: Implemented Migration 3 adding needs_stop_sync INTEGER DEFAULT 0 to SQLite. Native notification stops set this flag to 1. An automated reconciliation worker triggers on app resume, sync, and login to guarantee at-least-once stop delivery to the backend.
Defect P0-3: Fail-Closed Protection Against Hardware Refusal
Fixed
The Real-World Hazard: If Android denied location permissions or the phone’s GPS chip failed to start, the app previously reported “Journey Started Successfully” while silently capturing zero location points, creating a dangerous false sense of safety.
The Hardening Fix: Made startup strictly fail-closed. If native service initialization throws an error, the local session rolls back to ENDED and the app displays an honest, helpful alert: “Cannot Start Journey: Please enable GPS permissions.”

6.1 Shared SQLite Database Integrity

Both the native Kotlin Android service and the JavaScript React Native runtime communicate with the exact same physical database file (campussafe_offline.db). To eliminate concurrency lockups, we configured:

  • Write-Ahead Logging (WAL Mode): Allows simultaneous reading and writing without blocking queries.
  • 5,000ms Busy Timeout: Ensures native background threads wait patiently if a JavaScript transaction is momentarily committing.
  • Monotonic Sequence Allocation: Uses atomic SQLite transactions (last_seq_no + 1) to guarantee zero duplicate breadcrumb sequences.

Frequently Asked Questions • Non-Technical Guide

8 Frequently Asked Questions (Non-Technical Guide)

Here are simple, clear answers to common questions about CampusSafe for students, parents, and university administrators:

Q1: Will running this application drain my smartphone battery?
No. Unlike commercial tracker apps that ping GPS every single second, CampusSafe uses an intelligent 15-second batching window. When you tap “Stop Journey”, the background service powers off completely. Zero battery is consumed when you are not in an active journey.
Q2: Can a professor, warden, or administrator track me when I am in my hostel room?
Never. CampusSafe is built with zero passive surveillance. Tracking only exists when you explicitly tap “Start Safe Journey”. When you are resting, studying, or off-campus, the application has no access to your location whatsoever.
Q3: Can my classmates or friends see my live location on a map?
No. CampusSafe strictly bans classmate-to-classmate tracking to prevent peer stalking and unwanted social surveillance. Your live journey is only visible to LPU campus security officers and your verified parent/guardian.
Q4: What if I lose internet connectivity while walking?
You are 100% protected. Your phone acts as a secure local safe box. Every coordinate is recorded into the phone’s internal SQLite database. As soon as you enter a Wi-Fi zone or mobile network returns, the journey uploads automatically.
Q5: What happens if I press the SOS button in a basement with no signal?
Your phone immediately triggers a loud local siren to deter threats and locks your exact GPS coordinates into the emergency queue. The very second your phone detects even a faint cellular or Wi-Fi signal, the SOS packet is dispatched to the security control desk.
Q6: Can someone fool the attendance system using fake GPS apps?
No. The attendance engine uses Android’s hardware-level location accuracy indicators and cross-checks coordinates with spherical mathematical boundaries. Fake location mock providers are detected and rejected.
